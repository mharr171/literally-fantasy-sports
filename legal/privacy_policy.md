# Privacy Policy

**LiterallyFantasySports** · Effective date: November 1, 2026

LiterallyFantasySports ("we", "the game") is a free, browser-based sports management game at www.literallyfantasysports.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

- **Account information.** If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. If you sign in with an email link, we receive your email address.
- **Game data.** Your teams, players, lineups, match results, settings and anything else you create while playing.
- **Technical data.** Basic information your browser sends, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. We use it to keep the game running, prevent abuse and fix errors.

We do not collect payment information, and we do not ask for any information beyond what is listed here.

## How we use information

- To create and secure your account and sign you in.
- To run the game, including showing your team, results and leaderboard entries.
- To send sign-in emails. We do not send marketing emails.
- To find and fix problems and understand which features are used.

## What other players can see

Your team name, display name, season results and leaderboard entries are visible to other players. Finished seasons may appear in other players' leagues as computer-controlled "ghost" teams. Your email address is never shown to other players.

## Sharing

We do not sell your information and we do not show ads. We share information only with the services that run the game for us, and only so they can do so:

| Service | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Supabase | Database and sign-in |
| Vercel | Website hosting and page analytics |
| Google | "Continue with Google" sign-in |
| Resend | Sign-in emails |
| Sentry | Error reports |
| GitHub | Backups of game data, stored in a private repository |

We may also disclose information if required by law.

## Cookies

We use cookies and similar storage only to keep you signed in and to remember basic preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time by emailing admin@literallyfantasysports.com. After deletion, your past seasons may remain in other players' leagues as ghost teams with your name removed. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

## Children

The game is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

Data is stored with reputable providers, sent over encrypted connections, and protected by access rules. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and announce significant changes on the game's "What's new" page.

## Contact

Questions or requests: admin@literallyfantasysports.com
